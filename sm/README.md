## Video S1

Numerical simulations of a spherically-shaped nucleus in presence of spontaneous fluctuations and an external point-source force. Starting from the initial configuration of a perfect sphere, the video shows evolution of the shape over 3.0 min in two cases: a pushing force of magnitude `z_ext` applied at the south pole (magenta), and a pulling force of magnitude `z_ext` applied at the north pole (green). Parameters of the simulations (see [note.pdf](https://git.embl.org/rbelouso/hookeanity/-/tree/main/sm/note.pdf)): the equilibrium radius of the nucleus 3 µm, the dissipative constant 10 kg m<sup>-2</sup> µs<sup>-1</sup>, the Young modulus 5 kPa, the Poisson ratio 0.4, the effective temperature 80 aJ / k<sub>B</sub>, the external-force magnitude `z_ext` = 1.2 nN.

Video is provided in two formats: as animated PNG file [video_s1.png](https://git.embl.org/rbelouso/hookeanity/-/tree/main/sm/video_s1.png) and MP4 movie [video_s1.mp4](https://git.embl.org/rbelouso/hookeanity/-/tree/main/sm/video_s1.mp4).
